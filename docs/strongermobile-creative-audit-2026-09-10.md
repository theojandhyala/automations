# Deadset creative direction — Stronger reference audit

Date: 10 September 2026. Scope: Deadset only.

Status: reference research and production specification saved; not a deployed renderer change or a new batch of finished posts. Existing approved media, account routing and schedules were not changed by this audit.

## Owner direction

Match the understated, personal two-slide TikTok format of @strongermobile using Deadset's own screens and rights-cleared photos. This supersedes the earlier creative preference that every opener must show a person walking to a car. Preserve original wording, truthful product proof and licensing; do not duplicate their exact posts, download their photos, reproduce their results or copy their identity.

## Research coverage and limits

Inspected the supplied profile screenshot, the live public profile in Chrome, Latest and Popular grids, an initial 27-post caption/URL inventory, and eight individual reference posts spanning 2023–2026. Six were inspected on both actual slides; the older pinned and recovery examples were inspected through their cover plus actual product slide. This is a representative audit, not an exhaustive review of every historical upload. No account was logged into, followed, messaged or posted to during research.

The live profile displayed 76.6K followers and 9.5M likes. Those are profile totals, not evidence of app installs. The bio's million-user statement is the creator's claim, not independently verified.

## The repeatable format

**An ordinary moment → a short human setup → swipe → app evidence plus a short answer.**

- Two slides, not an educational five-card carousel.
- Slide one looks like a photo someone already had on their phone: gym mirror, floor/shoes/bottle, between-set moment, walk to the gym or casual car context.
- The photograph occupies the composition. There is no giant headline panel, feature badge or logo introduction.
- Short, sentence-case white sans-serif text with a black outline. Usually one or two lines in the upper-middle or middle, placed for the particular photo.
- The hook is an unfinished thought, quoted challenge or recognisable gym situation. It does not announce an app feature.
- Slide two uses an actual app screen. The extra copy is a reaction or answer, often one to six words, not a feature description.
- The inspected examples generally do **not** need arrows, circles, phone frames, benefit bullets or a third CTA slide. Those additions should not be the default.
- Short conversational caption and niche hashtags. The visible app identity and caption can carry the brand.
- Music supplies mood. Do not assume their chosen tracks are licensed for Deadset's Business account; check the allowed commercial route and do not promise exact-track selection if the publishing API only supports recommended music.

The result should feel like a gym post that happens to reveal Deadset, not a Deadset advert decorated with a gym photograph.

## Deadset proof must match the promise

The deployed playbook's `muscle_diagram` feature describes exercise targets, not measured strength development. It cannot honestly answer a twelve-week transformation hook simply because the silhouettes resemble Stronger's chart.

There is also an existing owner-provided **Deadset progress-comparison sample** documented in `assets/deadset-samples/DEADSET_PROGRESS_RECEIPTS_POST.md`. Its reviewed render shows Start/Now values, coloured body diagrams and a visible **SAMPLE PROFILE** label. This is a much closer visual reference, but is distinct from the exercise-target feature key. Preserve that label; do not turn the sample values into a first-person success story. Confirm the current screen, provenance and feature mapping before admitting it to the automated feature registry.

| Story type | Appropriate Deadset evidence | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Showing up consistently | Real training-history / heatmap screen | Only imply the consistency actually visible; sample history must remain labelled. |
| Getting stronger | Real PR wall, logged load history or a verified current progress-comparison screen | Never invent a gain, time interval or athlete result. |
| Having a plan | Current workout-plan screen | Show an actual plan, not implied coaching outcomes. |
| Remembering a session | Current logger or training history | Do not imply data was logged by the person pictured unless that is true. |
| Which muscle an exercise trains | Exercise-target muscle diagram | Do not reframe it as a before/after score. |
| Recovery humour | Only a verified Deadset recovery feature, if available | Stronger's recovery panel is not proof Deadset has the same capability. |

## Concrete production gaps found in this repository

1. `worker/src/lib/creative-playbooks.ts` hard-locks every Deadset opener to `deadset-casual-car-walk-v1` and explicitly rejects gym stock and key close-ups. Stronger's actual range is broader; prompt changes alone cannot solve this.
2. `worker/src/automations/tiktok-produce.ts` chooses its own fixed stock query, ignoring the model's photo query. Add an explicit, rights-safe, validated set of casual photo templates and rotate by story. Do not allow arbitrary unreviewed external images.
3. The producer sets `featureOverlay` to `contentLane.proofOverlay ?? feature.fallbackProofOverlay`, not the generated second-slide response. This explains why written reactions can become generic feature descriptions in final media. A future change must preserve and validate story-specific short answers, with safe fallbacks.
4. The heartbreak and villain lanes currently force the exercise-target `muscle_diagram` with a fixed answer. The disclaimer in feature truth does not make that visual implication appropriate. Remap the narrative to substantiating history or explicitly labelled demonstration evidence.
5. The current progress-sample render includes two circles and an arrow. For this new reference direction, default to **zero** callouts and one restrained reaction; make any annotation an exception justified by readability.
6. Template IDs participate in approval-time quality checks. Changing the identifier alone could block existing approved posts. Version the policy with compatibility for unchanged, already-approved media; do not rewrite approvals, destinations or in-flight delivery records.
7. A deterministic text score is not visual QA. Inspect full-size final slides and phone-size previews for natural source photos, legibility, truthful proof and matching setup/answer. Reject empty or misleading screens even if the text score passes.

## Application and measurement

- Treat this as the new Deadset art-direction reference, not as evidence that existing queued posts already match it.
- First build and inspect one complete two-slide master using a current verified screen and rights-cleared casual image. Then apply the validated composition to new production.
- Keep Cast's fishing rules and LifeScore's lock unchanged. Keep posting schedules, hard account routing, daily caps, safe retries and reconciliation untouched.
- Compare Deadset's own mature post results by hook family, opening-photo type and proof screen. Change one creative variable at a time. Do not infer performance from views alone or convert missing metrics to zero.
- Repetition of the grammar is deliberate; repetition of the identical photo, hook and payoff is not. Maintain the existing deduplication and source-variety safeguards.
- This audit did not create, approve or publish new media, modify production code, or deploy a Worker.
