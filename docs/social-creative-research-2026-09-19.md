## Concrete fixes

- Full-bleed 1080×1920 backgrounds; essential text/UI stay within safe margins.
- Deadset product master: larger exact official logo, deliberate black/red
  composition, authored benefit, actual daily-plan UI, visible example label,
  clearly separated App Store action. All elements centered at x=540.
- Producer previously replaced the authored product response with a fixed
  reaction. It now preserves authored short copy.
- Removed generated feature-label filler hooks. Finite fallbacks must stop when
  exhausted; a new suffix is not a new marketing concept.
- Removed conflicting gym/collage opener defaults and the Cast five-item claim.
  Deadset uses a candid person-led car/street hook. Cast is a hook, four distinct
  editorial items and a full branded final advertisement.
- Corrected a review-role bug: a per-image critic must assess the opening photo
  as the opening photo. It cannot fail slide 1 merely because the required
  slide-2 advertisement is not attached to that individual critique. Slide 2
  still requires the exact logo, real UI, benefit, branding and CTA. Scores,
  thresholds, source comparison, signatures and destination binding remain.
- Unrendered system drafts can render before review. Existing export holds,
  imported media, bookings and published posts remain protected.

## Next learning loop

Measure our own comparable 24–72-hour results by format, feature and source.
Views/likes are not retention or installs. Preserve missing metrics as unknown.
Record owner rejections even when the model passed. Inspect actual final
images, vary concepts rather than only photos, and use failures to revise the
brief and renderer. Never lower the release bar to fill five daily slots.
